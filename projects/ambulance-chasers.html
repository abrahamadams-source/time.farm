Ambulance Chasers, MIT Press, 2022

Ambulance Chasers is a series of photographic diptychs: on the left, a close-up on a personal-injury lawyer from a billboard; on the right, the landscape it is facing. A text by the art historian David Joselit runs alongside the series, and the book concludes with a long conversation between Adams and Joselit. MIT Press ↗
